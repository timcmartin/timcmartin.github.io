## Methods

### Turkey

- [Deconstructed Turkey](src/mains/deconstructed_turkey.md):
  - Froze carcass for later, used giblets and trim for [gravy](src/condiments/turkey_gravy.md#CIGravy).
  - Dry Brined 24 hours.
  - Roasted alone in the oven at 350 for 1.75 hours.
  - Used a half sheet pan lined with foil and a rack.
  - Turned out well.

- Used Dan's ATK Dry Brine:
- 4 Tbs salt
- 4 tsp sugar
- Turkey glove - separate skin from breasts and legs.
- use 4 tsp of mix on each breast, tsp each leg, rest inside the cavity.
- Let dry uncovered in the fridge on a rack in a baking sheet for 24 - 48
  hours (I did 24)

### Stuffing

- Used [this recipe](../sides/stuffing.md). It was excellent.
  - Did not grease dish, cooked for 45 minutes - was perfect, do not go a
    minute longer.
  - I put this in with about 30 minutes left of the turkey on the bottom rack
    and moved up after removing turkey with rolls.
