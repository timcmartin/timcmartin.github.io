# Sourdough Experiment

## Starters

- Night Before:
  - 25 g unfed starter
  - 50 g water
  - 50 g flour

- Morning:
  - 300 g water
  - 100 g starter (25 g back in the fridge)
  - 10 g salt
  - Stir to dissolve
  - 450 g flour

- Stir, shaggy dough, cover for 30 min.
- Stretch and Fold - 30 min rest
- Stretch and Fold - 4 - 8 hours rest (bulk fermentation).
- Preshape - 30 min rest.
- Shape -> final proof -> Banneton 1 hour on counter
- Rise for another 1/2 hour or cold proof into the fridge 8 - 12 hours.

- Preheat oven at 450.
- Bake cold from fridge.
- Lid on for 20 minutes.
- Remove lid and bake for another 15 - 20 minutes.
