# Quick Pickles

## Brine
* 4 cups white vinegar
* 4 Tablespoons Sugar
* 4 Tablespoons Salt

* Needs at least 20 minutes, can sit in fridge indefinitely.

## ATK Formula
* 1 lb of veg
* 1 1/2 c of water
* 1 1/2 c of vinegar
* 3 Tbs sugar
* 2 1/2 Tbs salt
* Bring to boil, let steep 10 min if adding aromatics
