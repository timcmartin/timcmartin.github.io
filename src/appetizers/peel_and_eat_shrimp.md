# Peel And Eat Shrimp

- Atco Blue Flame Kitchen

## Ingredients

- 2 pounds uncooked, thawed zipperback shrimp
- 1 lemon
- 1 package (74 g) Old Bay + more for finishing
- 8 cups of ice

## Directions

1. Scoop 8 cups ice into a large bowl; set aside.
2. Combine 6 cups water in a large saucepan with Old Bay.
   Cut lemon in half, squeeze juice into pot, and toss in lemon halves.
   Bring liquid to a boil then remove pot from heat.
   Add shell-on jumbo or large shrimp, deveined, and poach uncovered
   3 1/2 minutes or until cooked.
3. Immediately add reserved ice to saucepan to rapidly stop the cooking.
   Let shrimp sit in ice bath 10 minutes.
   Drain, pat dry, and toss with more Old Bay.
   Cover and chill until ready to serve with cocktail sauce.
